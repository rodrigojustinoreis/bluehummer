# Don't make me think (simplicity and glance test)

For every section, before shipping:

1. **Glance test (5 seconds)**: covering everything but the heading and the button, can a
   homeowner say what this section is about and what to do next? If not, rewrite the heading.
2. **One message, one CTA per section.** Primary CTA is always "Schedule a consultation". At most
   one secondary link.
3. **Text density**: section intro 1 to 2 sentences (under 40 words). Paragraphs under 60 words.
   Bullets over paragraphs for lists of facts. Cut anything that repeats another section.
4. **Obvious clickables**: links look like links, buttons look like buttons, no mystery icons.
5. **Navigation**: a visitor always knows where they are (active nav, breadcrumb) and can reach
   the consultation form in one click from any page.
6. **Mobile first**: the first screen on a 390 px phone shows the headline, one line of support
   and the CTA.
