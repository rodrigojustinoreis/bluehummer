# Section-by-section references (free galleries)

Before redesigning a section, open two or three examples from the matching free gallery, note
what makes the best ones work (layout, hierarchy, spacing), then design our own version against
`docs/brand.md`. Study patterns only; never copy code, text or images.

| Section | Free gallery |
|---|---|
| Navbar | https://navbar.gallery |
| Footer | https://www.footer.design |
| Call to action | https://www.cta.gallery |
| 404 page | https://www.404s.design |
| Any section, including bento grids for services | https://onsection.com |
| Whole pages (premium architecture/remodeler) | references in `design-brief.md` |

Notes per section:
- **Navbar**: logo left, 5 to 6 links, one primary button. Sticky with a thin border on scroll, no heavy shadow.
- **Footer**: service area list, contact slots, legal links; navy background; no repeated disclaimers.
- **CTA**: one sentence of value, one button, optional photo. No countdowns or urgency gimmicks.
- **404**: big serif "404", one helpful sentence, a short list of the most useful pages, consultation button (done 2026-11-18; refine visually).
- **Services bento**: kitchen as the large tile with photo, bath and lower levels as smaller tiles; each tile one line plus link. Avoid three identical cards.
