Source: Semrush Keyword Overview (Bulk analysis), US database, looked up by the owner in the Semrush web interface on 2026-11-22: 96 terms, then a complementary sample of 24 Northern Virginia terms, 120 unique terms in total (most rows "updated 1 month"). The Semrush API returned error 132 (no API units) that day; it did not affect the interface lookup. Values are copied unchanged.

## How to read the numbers

- **Volume is the US monthly search volume for the exact phrase**, not searches made only by people living in that city. Do not add rows together and do not treat them as leads or unique people.
- **0, n/a or "no data" does not mean no demand.** Small places often fall under Semrush's reporting threshold. KD 0 with a real results page can be genuine.
- **North Bethesda, North Potomac, Fulton and Clarksburg pages exist for coverage** (they are inside the service area), not because the data shows high demand.
- No number from this file is used in customer-facing copy (no volumes, counts, ranks or "high demand" wording).
