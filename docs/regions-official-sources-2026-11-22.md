# Permit Authority and Local Rules by Jurisdiction (official sources only)

Prepared for Blue Hammer Remodeling local area pages. All URLs accessed **2026-11-22**.

**How to read this report**
- Every fact comes from an official government or authority site (.gov, county/city/town, WSSC, MD Labor, DPOR, DLCP, Columbia Association).
- **Verification level:** "Direct" means the page itself was opened and read. "Excerpt" means the fact comes from the official page's text as returned by search; the page could not be opened directly (some sites, such as gaithersburgmd.gov and dlcp.dc.gov, block automated fetches) or was not opened. Recheck "Excerpt" items in a browser before publishing.
- Fees, timelines and thresholds change. Do not publish dollar figures without rechecking them.
- "Not found" means no official statement was located. It does not mean the rule doesn't exist.

## 1. Statewide and contractor licensing rules

### Maryland: MHIC license
- **Fact:** Any contractor who solicits or performs home improvement work in Maryland must hold a Maryland Home Improvement Commission (MHIC) license. This covers homes and buildings with three or fewer units, and it also applies to out-of-state contractors. (Excerpt)
- URL: https://labor.maryland.gov/license/mhic/mhicfaqlic.shtml and https://www.labor.maryland.gov/license/mhic/mhiclicreq.shtml
- **Fact:** Montgomery County DPS states: "A contractor must have a Maryland Home Improvement Commission (MHIC) license to obtain a permit." Homeowners may apply in their own name. (Direct)
- URL: https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/department-permitting-services/all-permits/residential-permits/residential-alterations-permit-process
- **Fact:** Baltimore County requires contractors doing residential alterations to supply their Maryland Home Improvement License number. (Excerpt)
- URL: https://www.baltimorecountymd.gov/departments/pai/application/addition-alteration-modification

### Washington, DC: DLCP Home Improvement Contractor license
- **Fact:** DLCP guidance states that all home improvement contractors are legally required to have a Home Improvement Contractor (HIC) Basic Business License, plus a Home Improvement Salesperson (HIS) license or a designated salesperson. Listed requirements include a $25,000 surety bond, liability insurance, and Clean Hands certification. Applications are filed online through BOSS. (Excerpt: dlcp.dc.gov returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch.)
- URLs: https://dlcp.dc.gov/node/1618836 ; https://dlcp.dc.gov/page/contractor-and-construction-services ; https://dlcp.dc.gov/node/1832226
- **Uncertainty:** DLCP pages list different fees ($288.20 on one page; $99 for 2 years or $198 for 4 years on another). Do not publish a fee.

### Virginia: DPOR contractor license classes
- **Fact (current, per DPOR consumer sheet "Revised 4/29/2025 (effective 07/01/2025)"):** Class C covers a single project over $1,000 and under $30,000, or less than $250,000 in total over 12 months. Class B covers $30,000 to under $150,000 per project, or $250,000 to under $1 million per year. Class A covers $150,000 or more per project, or $1 million or more per year. (Excerpt)
- URL: https://www.dpor.virginia.gov/sites/default/files/boards/Contractors/Contractor_Consumer_Info.pdf
- **Fact:** DPOR's step-by-step guide says the Qualified Individual for the **home improvement specialty** must pass the home improvement technical examination. (Direct, PDF text) **Caution:** the same guide still shows the older class limits (Class C under $10,000; Class B under $120,000). Those limits are superseded.
- URL: https://www.dpor.virginia.gov/sites/default/files/boards/Contractors/Contractors_Step_Guide.pdf
- **Uncertainty:** The exact regulation effective date was not confirmed in the regulation text. The Board for Contractors main page (https://www.dpor.virginia.gov/Boards/Contractors, Direct) does not list the thresholds.

## 2. Montgomery County, MD

### Default authority: Montgomery County Department of Permitting Services (DPS)
- **Fact:** DPS requires a permit before renovating or reconstructing an existing structure, but not for repairs. Painting, wallpapering, replacing a faucet, and installing countertops, hardwood, tile or carpet need no permit if no structural changes are made. (Direct)
- **Fact:** The page also says: "You may also need a permit or other permission if you live a Montgomery County municipality that requires it." It does not list which municipalities. (Direct)
- **Fact:** "WSSC is an outside agency that must complete their review before your permit may be issued." (Direct)
- URL: https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/department-permitting-services/all-permits/residential-permits/residential-alterations-permit-process
- **Applies to (unincorporated, no municipal permit office found):** Germantown, North Bethesda, North Potomac, Darnestown, Clarksburg, Bethesda, Potomac, Olney. These are unincorporated places, so the county DPS is the building permit authority. No official page was found that lists them one by one. The conclusion follows from the absence of any municipal government for these places.
- **Uncertainty:** The DPS "Towns and Municipalities" page (https://montgomerycountymd.gov/DPS/municipalities.html, Direct) did not state which municipalities issue their own permits.

### City of Gaithersburg (issues its own permits)
- **Fact:** The City's Planning and Code Administration issues Residential Improvement Permits for finishing a basement, remodeling kitchens and bathrooms, changing a room to a bedroom, and enlarging windows or doors. Contact: 301-258-6330, permitting@gaithersburgmd.gov. (Excerpt: site returns 403 to automated fetch.)
- URL: https://www.gaithersburgmd.gov/services/permitting-inspections/residential-permits/improvement-permits
- **Fact:** The City also requires a residential electrical permit for all electrical work, and electricians must be licensed with the City. Anyone doing plumbing or gasfitting in the City must be licensed with WSSC. (Excerpt)
- URLs: https://www.gaithersburgmd.gov/services/permitting-inspections/residential-permits/electrical-permits ; https://www.gaithersburgmd.gov/services/licensing
- **Fact:** The City asks for HOA approval correspondence before it issues a building permit, where applicable. (Excerpt)
- URL: https://www.gaithersburgmd.gov/services/permitting-inspections/residential-permits
- **Historic:** The City has its own Historic District Commission. A City Historic Area Work Permit is required before exterior changes to locally designated properties or to buildings in its two local districts, "Brookes, Russell and Walker" and "Chestnut/Meem". There is no application fee. (Excerpt)
- URL: https://www.gaithersburgmd.gov/services/planning-services/historic-preservation/historic-area-work-permit-applications
- **Uncertainty:** No official page was found that states whether plumbing permits in Gaithersburg are issued by WSSC (likely, given WSSC's two-county jurisdiction, but not confirmed).

### City of Rockville (issues its own permits)
- **Fact:** Rockville's Inspection Services Division reviews and approves building permits "for all construction projects in City", from additions to fences, sheds and decks, before construction starts. Contact: 240-314-8240, permits@rockvillemd.gov, 111 Maryland Ave. (Excerpt)
- URL: https://www.rockvillemd.gov/2180/Permits-Inspections-Division
- **Fact (plumbing/gas):** WSSC is the code authority for gas installations in Montgomery and Prince George's Counties "with the exception of the Cities of Rockville and Poolesville". The King Farm section of Rockville is under WSSC jurisdiction. Plumbers must be licensed in the City of Rockville to secure a plumbing permit. For water heaters, Rockville directs applicants to check the water service provider map: WSSC-served addresses get the permit from WSSC. (Excerpt, from WSSC code PDF and Rockville documents)
- URLs: https://www.wsscwater.com/sites/default/files/2025-11/2024_WSSC_Plumbing_and_Fuel_Gas_Code.pdf ; https://rockvillemd.gov/DocumentCenter/View/527/Commercial-and-Residential-Plumbing-Permit-Application ; https://www.rockvillemd.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Express-Water-Heater-Permit-Guide.pdf
- **Historic:** Rockville has its own Historic District Commission (HDC). Owners of property in a Historic District Zone must get a Certificate of Approval before exterior alterations or demolition. Repairs and replacements with identical materials generally do not need one. (Excerpt)
- URLs: https://www.rockvillemd.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Technical-Guides-for-Exterior-Alterations-Exterior-Siding.pdf ; https://www.rockvillemd.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Technical-Guides-for-Exterior-Alterations-Roofing.pdf

### Town of Kensington (Town permit plus County permit)
- **Fact:** Town FAQ: "Do I need a Building Permit? In most cases, yes. Please check with the Town Hall staff to determine the type of permit you may need." (Direct)
- URL: https://tok.md.gov/resident-faqs/
- **Fact:** Town newsletters state that both a Town and a County permit are needed for any interior or exterior structural changes, except landscaping and general repairs. Historic District properties also require a HAWP. (Excerpt, from newsletters dated about 2018 to 2022)
- URL: https://tok.md.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/December-2018-1.pdf
- **Historic:** Kensington is a Montgomery County historic district (see section 2, HAWP).

### Town of Garrett Park (Town permit plus County permit)
- **Fact:** A Town of Garrett Park permit is required for new structures, additions, decks, porches and demolition, as well as driveways and curb cuts. Apply for the Town permit before the Montgomery County permit, except when a HAWP is required. When a HAWP is required, it must be obtained before the County will process the building permit. (Excerpt)
- URL: https://www.garrettparkmd.gov/government/operations/permits/
- **Historic:** The Town says the district was placed on the County Master Plan for Historic Preservation in 1992. (Excerpt)
- **Uncertainty:** No Garrett Park statement was found on whether interior-only remodels need a Town permit.

### Chevy Chase (several separate municipalities)
- **Chevy Chase Village:** Village Code Sec. 8-5 says applicants must first obtain any required County Building Permit, and then any required Village Building Permit. The Village's user guide says it "does not require a Village building permit if you are doing work only to the interior of your home," although other Village permits may still apply. A HAWP must be issued before a Village building permit. (Excerpt)
- URLs: https://www.chevychasevillagemd.gov/DocumentCenter/View/125/CCV-Users-Guide-to-Local-Permitting-Process--2022-PDF ; https://chevychasevillagemd.gov/DocumentCenter/View/4246/Chapter-8-BUILDINGS-AND-BUILDING-REGULATIONS0122
- **Town of Chevy Chase:** "Most construction projects require that building permits be obtained from both Montgomery County and the Town of Chevy Chase." The Town issues its permit after the County permit. Larger projects (more than 500 sq ft added) require pre-application consultations. (Excerpt)
- URL: https://www.townofchevychase.org/234/Building-Permitting ; https://townofchevychase.org/DocumentCenter/View/873/Building-Permit-Process
- **Other Chevy Chase municipalities:** Chevy Chase Section 3, Chevy Chase Section 5, Martin's Additions, North Chevy Chase, and Chevy Chase View are separate incorporated municipalities. Martin's Additions publishes its own permit rules, and Chevy Chase View has a 2024 permit procedures document. Specific rules for Section 3, Section 5 and North Chevy Chase: **not found.**
- URLs: https://martinsadditions.org/how_do_i/apply_for/how_do_i/permits.php ; https://chevychaseviewmd.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/permitproceduresrevision2024.pdf
- **Uncertainty:** "Chevy Chase" mailing addresses also cover unincorporated areas served only by DPS. A page cannot assume a single authority for "Chevy Chase".

### City of Takoma Park (County issues permits; City letter required)
- **Fact:** Building permits for construction, renovation or demolition in Takoma Park are issued by Montgomery County DPS, not the City. Any project that needs a County permit "first requires a Municipality Letter from Takoma Park". The letter is free and is not a permit. The City separately regulates trees, stormwater and work in the City right-of-way. Historic district properties also need a County HAWP. (Direct)
- URL: https://takomaparkmd.gov/398/Municipality-Letters-for-Construction-Re
- **Note:** The brief assumed Takoma Park issues its own permits. The official City page says it does not.

### WSSC plumbing and gas permits
- **Fact:** WSSC Water's Plumbing and Fuel Gas Code requires a licensed plumber or gasfitter for most major plumbing projects in Prince George's and Montgomery Counties. Homeowners may do limited work, such as leak repairs. (Direct)
- URL: https://www.wsscwater.com/work-with-us/plumbing-and-inspections/plumbing-fuel-gas-inspections/plumbing-fuel-gas-inspections
- **Fact:** WSSC permits must be applied for by a WSSC-registered Master Plumber/Gasfitter (or proxy, or a Maryland PE). Gas appliances require permits and inspections, and homeowners may not install gas appliances themselves. (Excerpt)
- URLs: https://www.wsscwater.com/gasappliances ; https://www.wsscwater.com/work-with-us/permit-services/information-homeowners
- **Exceptions:** The City of Rockville (except King Farm) and Poolesville (see Rockville above). (Excerpt, WSSC code)

### Montgomery County historic preservation (HPC / HAWP)
- **Fact:** Exterior work on properties listed on the Montgomery County Master Plan for Historic Preservation requires approval from the Montgomery County Historic Preservation Commission through the Historic Area Work Permit (HAWP) process. (Direct)
- **Fact:** Districts named on the HPC design guidelines page: Boyds, Brookeville, Capitol View, Cedar Grove, Chevy Chase Village, Clarksburg, Damascus-Goshen, Garrett Park, Germantown, Greenwich Forest, Hawkins Lane, Hyattstown, Kensington, Linden Lane/Forest Glen, Potomac Overlook, Sandy Spring/Ashton, Somerset, Takoma Park, Upper Patuxent/Etchison. (Direct)
- URL: https://montgomeryplanning.org/planning/historic/historic-area-work-permits/design-guidelines/
- **Places from the brief with a County historic district:** Kensington, Garrett Park, Chevy Chase Village, Takoma Park, Clarksburg, Germantown, Potomac Overlook (a district in the Potomac area; exact location not verified).
- **Places from the brief with no County historic district on that list:** Olney, Bethesda, North Bethesda, North Potomac, Darnestown. Individually designated Master Plan sites can still exist anywhere.
- **Uncertainty:** That page lists districts that have design guidelines. It may not be the complete official list. The full list is in the Master Plan for Historic Preservation: https://montgomeryplanning.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Master-Plan-for-Historic-Preservation.pdf. Clarksburg and Germantown districts cover small historic cores, not the whole community.

## 3. Howard County, MD (Columbia, Ellicott City, Clarksville, Fulton)

### Howard County Department of Inspections, Licenses and Permits (DILP)
- **Fact:** DILP issues residential building permits countywide. Howard County has no incorporated municipalities, so Columbia, Ellicott City, Clarksville and Fulton are all covered by DILP. Owner-occupants of one- and two-family homes may act as their own general contractor only for their primary residence. Electronic submission is required for residential building permits. DILP is at 3430 Courthouse Drive, Ellicott City; phone 410-313-2455. (Excerpt)
- URLs: https://www.howardcountymd.gov/inspections-licenses-permits ; https://www.howardcountymd.gov/permits ; https://www.howardcountymd.gov/inspections-licenses-permits/forms-fees
- **Uncertainty:** The statement that Howard County has no incorporated municipalities was not confirmed on an official page during this research.

### Ellicott City and Lawyers Hill historic districts: Howard County Historic Preservation Commission
- **Fact:** Exterior alterations in a Howard County historic district (the application form covers Ellicott City and Lawyers Hill) need a Certificate of Approval from the Historic Preservation Commission before work begins. Interior changes and routine maintenance do not (County Code Sec. 16.609). Applications are due at least 22 days before the meeting. (Excerpt)
- URLs: https://www.howardcountymd.gov/historic-preservation-commission/resource/view-certificate-approval-and-tax-credit-pre-approval ; https://www.howardcountymd.gov/planning-zoning/resource/lawyers-hill-guidelines

### Columbia: Columbia Association and village architectural covenants
- **Fact:** Each Columbia village's covenants are recorded in the Howard County land records and attach to every property in that village. Owners apply to their **village** for property alterations, and the village reviews the application against its architectural covenants. Since 2018, village staff also notify Columbia Association so property-boundary compliance can be reviewed. (Excerpt)
- URLs: https://columbiaassociation.org/explore-columbia/columbia-villages/ ; https://columbiaassociation.org/blog/your-community/columbia-association-working-to-protect-open-space-from-property-encroachments/ ; https://www.columbiaassociation.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/6792.pdf
- **Fact:** CA's Architectural Resource Committee is a CA Board committee that deals with properties in violation of their village covenants. It is not the body that first reviews alterations. (Excerpt)
- URL: https://columbiaassociation.org/governance/leadership/board-of-directors/committees/arc/
- **Not found:** Village application forms, fees, and timelines. These are published by each village association.

## 4. Washington, DC

### DC Department of Buildings (DOB)
- **Fact:** The DOB Alteration & Repair permit covers any construction or renovation to an existing structure, including reconfiguring space, in-kind replacement and repairs. One- and two-family projects use the DOB Permit Wizard. The Homeowner's Center helps owners of one- and two-family homes and can advise whether a permit is needed. Some simple jobs qualify for online Instant Permits. (Excerpt)
- URLs: https://dob.dc.gov/service/alteration-repair-permit ; https://dob.dc.gov/page/homeowners-center ; https://dob.dc.gov/instantpermits
- **Uncertainty:** DOB documents list different square-foot limits for interior work handled through simpler review (500 sq ft on one page, 750 or 1,000 sq ft in a service-level PDF). Do not publish a threshold.

### DC historic districts: Historic Preservation Office (Office of Planning)
- **Fact:** Historic preservation review applies when a building permit is required for work that affects the exterior appearance of a historic property. HPO clearance of the building permit is the preservation approval; there is no separate certificate. Interior-only work that needs a permit does not trigger HPO review on its own. Postcard (instant) permits are not available for historic properties. Major projects go to the Historic Preservation Review Board. (Excerpt)
- URLs: https://planning.dc.gov/page/building-permits-historic-property ; https://planning.dc.gov/page/hpo-expedited-review

### DC contractor license (DLCP)
- See section 1. A Home Improvement Contractor license from DLCP is required. (Excerpt)

## 5. Northern Virginia

### Fairfax County Land Development Services (LDS): McLean, Great Falls, and "Fairfax" addresses outside the City
- **Fact:** Fairfax County LDS lists kitchen renovations as interior alterations that require a permit. Plumbing, electrical and gas work also need permits. Replacing cabinets or countertops, replacing flooring, and painting do not. A finished basement needs a residential interior alteration building permit and an electrical permit, plus a plumbing permit if a bath or bar sink is added. (Excerpt)
- URLs: https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/landdevelopment/when-permit-required ; https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/landdevelopment/finished-basements ; https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/landdevelopment/permit-library/addition-alteration-residential
- **Applies to:** McLean and Great Falls (unincorporated Fairfax County). Fairfax County mailing addresses outside the City of Fairfax and outside the towns are also covered by LDS. No official page was found that names McLean or Great Falls explicitly; the conclusion follows from their unincorporated status.
- **Fact:** The county's permit application says that permit applications in the Town of Vienna must start at Vienna Town Hall. (Excerpt)
- URL: https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/landdevelopment/sites/landdevelopment/files/assets/documents/forms/bpa.pdf
- **Uncertainty:** "Fairfax" mailing addresses span the City and the County. The county says to confirm addresses with its Master Address Repository: https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/landdevelopment/addressing-and-street-names

### Town of Vienna
- **Fact:** Fairfax County serves as the building official for the Town of Vienna and issues building permits. The Town's Planning and Zoning department reviews Fairfax County building permit applications for compliance with the Town Zoning Code (Chapter 18). Applicants file with the County first, then with the Town, using the County application number. Fences, patios and driveways need a Town zoning permit. (Excerpt)
- URLs: https://www.viennava.gov/Town-Departments/Planning-Zoning/Permits-Applications/Residential-Projects ; https://www.viennava.gov/Town-Departments/Planning-Zoning/Permits-Applications

### City of Fairfax (independent city, issues its own permits)
- **Fact:** The City of Fairfax is an independent city, "not a part of any county". (Excerpt)
- URL: https://www.fairfaxva.gov/Government/About-the-City
- **Fact:** City of Fairfax Code Administration issues residential building permits (demolition, new construction, additions, alterations). Trade permits are filed separately and reference the building permit number. A zoning permit must be approved before the building permit is issued. Contact: 703-385-7830. (Excerpt)
- URLs: https://www.fairfaxva.gov/Property-Business/Permit-License-Hub/Permit-Applications/Building-Permit-Residential ; https://www.fairfaxva.gov/Government/Departments/Fire-Department/Code-Administration

### Arlington County
- **Fact:** Arlington County requires a residential building permit for interior alterations that affect gas or oil appliances, pipes or drywall. Replacing cabinets, appliances (not gas or oil fueled) or fixtures in the exact same location, without moving pipes or drywall, needs no permit. Separate plumbing/gas permits apply to adding, removing or relocating fixtures or piping. (Excerpt)
- URLs: https://www.arlingtonva.us/Government/Programs/Building/Permits/Residential ; https://www.arlingtonva.us/Government/Programs/Building/Permits/Plumbing-Gas ; https://www.arlingtonva.us/Government/Programs/Sustainability/AIRE/Arlington-Home-Energy-Hub/Understand-Permitting-Requirements

### Owings Mills, MD (Baltimore County)
- **Fact:** Baltimore County has no incorporated towns or cities, so Owings Mills (unincorporated) falls under Baltimore County government. (Excerpt)
- URLs: https://countycouncil.baltimorecountymd.gov/about/ ; https://2013mdmanual.msa.maryland.gov/msa/mdmanual/36loc/bco/html/bcomu.html
- **Fact:** Baltimore County Permits, Approvals and Inspections (PAI) requires an Addition, Alteration, Structural Modification permit for alterations, including adding a bedroom or kitchen. Siding or roof repair does not need a permit. Exterior work on historic district or Landmarks List properties does. Contractors must provide their MHIC number. (Excerpt)
- URL: https://www.baltimorecountymd.gov/departments/pai/application/addition-alteration-modification

## 7. Additional Northern Virginia places (coordinator final additions)

### Alexandria, VA
- **Fact:** The City of Alexandria Permit Center (Code Administration, 703-746-4200) handles building permits inside the city. For properties in a historic district or listed as a 100-Year-Old Building, the applicant must get Board of Architectural Review (BAR) approval before applying for any permit, and must upload BAR-stamped plans with the permit application. (Excerpt)
- URL: https://www.alexandriava.gov/sites/default/files/2022-05/New%20Residential%20Plan%20Submittal%20Requirements.pdf
- **Historic:** In the Old and Historic Alexandria District, a Certificate of Appropriateness is required for new construction and exterior changes visible from the public right-of-way. Interior work does not need BAR approval. A Permit to Demolish is required for demolition or capsulation of more than 25 sq ft, regardless of visibility. Preservation staff: 703-746-3833. (Excerpt)
- URLs: https://www.alexandriava.gov/Preservation ; https://www.alexandriava.gov/sites/default/files/2024-01/Annual%202024%20Historic%20District%20Ltr%20FINAL.pdf
- **"Alexandria" addresses outside the city:** Not found as an explicit official statement. Alexandria-mailing-address areas outside the city limits are in unincorporated Fairfax County, so Fairfax County LDS applies (section 5). This is inferred from jurisdiction, not stated on a page. Verify by parcel.
- **Uncertainty:** The City of Alexandria's independent-city status was not separately confirmed on an alexandriava.gov page in this research.

### Falls Church, VA
- **Fact:** The City of Falls Church Building Safety Division (300 Park Avenue, Suite 103E; 703-248-5080; permits@fallschurchva.gov) issues building permits in the city. Permits are needed to change a wall, move or add plumbing or electrical fixtures, and for any gas work. Painting, flooring, and cabinet replacement generally need none. (Excerpt)
- URLs: https://www.fallschurchva.gov/1914/When-a-Building-Permit-is-Required ; https://www.fallschurchva.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1102/Brochure-1-Building-Permit-Fees-and-Process-Summary
- **"Falls Church" addresses outside the city:** Not found as an explicit official statement. These areas are in Fairfax County, so Fairfax County LDS applies (section 5). This is inferred, not stated on a page. Verify by parcel.

### Reston, VA
- **Building permits:** Fairfax County LDS (Reston is unincorporated; section 5). No page names Reston explicitly.
- **Fact:** The Reston Association Design Review Board is "responsible for reviewing exterior alterations/improvements of properties within Reston Association." Some changes need no application. Minor items (colors, fences, lights) are usually handled by Covenants staff, and larger projects (screened porches, sunrooms, major additions) go to a DRB panel. Work may not start until the final written decision is issued. Covenants Administration: 703-435-6530. (Excerpt)

### Herndon, VA
- **Fact:** The Town of Herndon's Building Inspections division (Public Works) issues building permits for the town itself, through its Cityworks system, under the Virginia USBC. Additions, alterations, decks and garages generally need a permit. Projects needing Fire Marshal approval are referred to Fairfax County. A Town business license is required before applying. Contact: 703-435-6850. (Excerpt)

- **Note:** Herndon is the opposite of Vienna. Herndon issues its own building permits, while Vienna relies on Fairfax County.
