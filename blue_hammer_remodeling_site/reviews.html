How to vet any remodeler in Maryland

  • License. Look the contractor up in the Maryland Home Improvement Commission’s public license search, by name or license number.
  • Insurance. Ask for a current certificate of insurance.
  • A written scope. Line items, allowances, the payment schedule and how changes are handled, before you sign.
  • Permits in writing. The agreement should say which permits the work needs and who applies for each.
  • The person responsible. Confirm these details with the person who signs your contract.
