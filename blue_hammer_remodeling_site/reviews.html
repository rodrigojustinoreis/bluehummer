How to vet any remodeler in Maryland

  • MHIC license. Look the number up on the Maryland Home Improvement Commission site and confirm it is active.
  • Insurance certificates. Ask for current general liability and workers’ compensation certificates naming you.
  • A written scope. Line items, allowances, payment schedule, change-order process and warranty, before any deposit.
  • Permits in their name. The contractor, not the homeowner, should pull the permit and schedule inspections.
  • References you can call. Recent projects of similar size in similar houses.
