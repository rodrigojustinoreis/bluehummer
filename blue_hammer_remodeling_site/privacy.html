Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Blue Hammer Remodeling (“we”, “us”) respects the information you share when you ask about a residential remodeling project. This policy explains what we collect through bluehammermd.com, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you submit our consultation form we collect your first and last name, email address, telephone number, project ZIP code, the type of project, your preferred timeline, any message you write, your consent to be contacted, the page the form was sent from and, if you arrived from a link that included them, campaign tags such as utm_source. Our hosting provider also records standard technical data (IP address, browser type, pages requested) in server logs needed to operate and protect the website.

How we use it

We use your information to review your inquiry, confirm whether your home is inside our service area, contact you about your project, prepare proposals and keep records of our conversations. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Where submissions are stored

The website is hosted on Netlify. Form submissions are processed and stored by Netlify Forms, where we review them, and may be forwarded to our business inbox by email notification. Netlify applies automated spam filtering to submissions. Netlify’s own privacy practices are described in its privacy policy.

Analytics, cookies and third-party content

This website does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts, and it sets no marketing cookies. All images and fonts are served from our own domain. If we add analytics or other tools in the future, we will update this policy before they go live.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect inquiries, including HTTPS for every page and access limited to the people who handle project requests. No system can guarantee absolute security. We keep inquiry records for as long as needed to respond to you, to manage a project you hire us for and to meet legal and accounting obligations, and we delete or anonymize them afterward.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information you submitted at any time, and you can withdraw consent to be contacted by telling us so in any reply. Use our contact page to reach us.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
