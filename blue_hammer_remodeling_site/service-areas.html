Which permit office covers which address

A short guide to who reviews remodeling work. Each town page has the detail and the links for its area.

  • Montgomery County. The Department of Permitting Services issues the County building permit. Plumbing and gas go through WSSC Water.
  • Towns and villages in the County. The County’s list of municipalities shows which ones require their own approval in addition to the County permit, and in what order.
  • City of Rockville. Properties in the City of Rockville get their permits from the City. A Rockville mailing address is not always inside the city limits, so the address is checked first.
  • Howard County. The Department of Inspections, Licenses and Permits issues building, plumbing, electrical and HVAC permits.

What to send with your address

Four details are enough to tell whether the project is one we can take.

  • The street address. It shows which county, city or town reviews the work.
  • The room and the change you want. A kitchen, a bathroom, a basement or another interior room, or a small repair.
  • Water and sewer. Say whether the house is on public service or has a well or a septic system.
  • Budget range and timing. Both shape the scope from the first conversation.

Which parts of Montgomery County do you cover?

We are based in Olney and have pages for Olney, Bethesda, Chevy Chase, Kensington and Garrett Park, Potomac, Rockville and Silver Spring. Other Montgomery County addresses are confirmed one by one. Enter your ZIP code in the consultation form to check yours.

Do you work in Howard County?

Yes, in Clarksville, Highland, Dayton, Glenelg, Glenwood, Fulton, West Friendship and Ellicott City. Howard County has its own permit office, so the Howard County page covers it separately from the Montgomery County towns.

Do you work in Washington, DC or Northern Virginia?

The service area reaches about 40 miles from Olney, which takes in Washington, DC and part of Northern Virginia. Send the address before planning anything. Whether we can take a project there is confirmed for that jurisdiction before any agreement.

Which office issues the permit for my address?

It follows the address. In Montgomery County it is the Department of Permitting Services, and some towns and villages add their own approval. Inside the City of Rockville it is the City. In Howard County it is the Department of Inspections, Licenses and Permits.
