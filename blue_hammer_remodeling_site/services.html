ProjectTypical scopePlanning range

Ranges reflect typical total project cost including design, permits, labor and materials for homes in Olney, Potomac, Bethesda, Chevy Chase, Kensington, Rockville and the Howard County corridor. Older homes and homes on well and septic can add engineering, electrical service or septic review costs; we identify those in the walkthrough.

How a project runs.

Design first, then a written scope, then construction.

    Layouts and drawings first, then cabinetry and finishes chosen together.

  3. 3

    Scope, price & permits

    A written proposal and the permit application for your jurisdiction.

  4. 4

    Floor protection during the work and a final punch-list walk-through.
