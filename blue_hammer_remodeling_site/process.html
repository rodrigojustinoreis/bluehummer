Step by step

From first idea to a defined project.

The exact sequence depends on the service and scope. The goal is to make sure expectations are clear before construction begins.

A note on timelines

Exact start dates, completion dates, permitting requirements, and project costs are established case by case—not promised before scope review.

1

Start the conversation

Share your project type, ZIP code, desired timeline, and the result you are hoping for.

2

Understand the property and scope

Discuss the existing conditions, the changes you want, and any details that may affect planning.

3

Define the plan

Talk through materials, design priorities, logistics, and whether permits or specialty trades may be required.

4

Review the proposed agreement

Confirm the written scope, schedule expectations, and payment terms before work begins.

5

Coordinate the project

Carry out the agreed process with clear milestones and communication.

6

Review the finished work

Walk through the result against the agreed scope and discuss any remaining items.
