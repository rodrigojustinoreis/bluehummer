Baltimore County reviews an Owings Mills remodel

Baltimore County has no incorporated towns, so the County is the permit office.

  • Permits. Baltimore County Permits, Approvals and Inspections issues the alteration permit, including for work that adds a bedroom or a kitchen.
  • Historic properties. Exterior work on a property in a County historic district or on its Landmarks List also needs a permit.
