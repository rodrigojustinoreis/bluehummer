Wells, septic and the County

Darnestown is unincorporated, so Montgomery County is the permit office.

  • Building permit. The County Department of Permitting Services reviews interior alterations.
  • Well or septic. Before adding bedrooms or plumbing on a property with a well or septic system, the County checks the system. Its well and septic guide explains which projects need that review.
  • Historic sites. If the property is designated historic, exterior work may need a Historic Area Work Permit. The Planning map shows designations.

What to tell us about a Darnestown house

Send the address, the room and whether the house is on a well, septic or public service. Photos of the room and the equipment area help us plan the first visit.

Does a well or septic system change the project?

It can, when the work adds a bedroom or plumbing. The County reviews the system first; its guide explains when.

Can we add a bathroom to the lower level?

That depends on the drain route, which is checked first. On septic, the County’s review is part of the plan.

Who issues the permit in Darnestown?

Montgomery County’s Department of Permitting Services. Darnestown is not an incorporated town.
