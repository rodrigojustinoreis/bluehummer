General information

This website provides general information about Blue Hammer Remodeling and lets homeowners ask about residential repair and remodeling projects in Maryland, Washington, DC and Virginia. Nothing on the site is a binding offer to perform work or a guarantee of availability.

Consultations and estimates

Submitting a consultation request does not create a contract. Scope, pricing, schedule, permit responsibilities, licensing and insurance documentation, payment terms and warranty are confirmed in a written agreement before any work begins.

Cost ranges on this site

Where we publish cost ranges, they are planning ranges for typical projects in established Montgomery and Howard County homes, offered to help you set expectations. They are not quotes. The price for your project depends on its design, selections and existing conditions and is provided in writing after a walkthrough and design review.

Design images

Unless an image is expressly labeled as a completed Blue Hammer Remodeling project, images on this website are illustrative design imagery showing styles and finish levels. They do not depict our completed work, our team or our clients.

Service area and licensing

We work within about 40 miles of Olney, MD, in Maryland, Washington, DC and Virginia. Service availability can change based on scheduling, scope and jurisdiction. Maryland home improvement contractors must hold a Maryland Home Improvement Commission (MHIC) license; DC and Virginia have their own contractor licensing; verify any contractor’s license for your jurisdiction before signing a contract.

Website accuracy

We work to keep the site accurate and current, but information may change without notice. External links are provided for convenience and we are not responsible for third-party content.
