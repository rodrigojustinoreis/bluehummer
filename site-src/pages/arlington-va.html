Kitchen, bath and basement in Arlington

What to decide first in each room.

Kitchen remodeling

Decide whether appliances and the sink stay put. Arlington County says replacing cabinets and fixtures in the same place, without moving pipes or drywall, needs no permit; moving them does.

Bathroom remodeling

Keeping the toilet, shower and sink in place is the smaller project. Moving them adds new drains and supply lines and a plumbing permit.

Basements and interiors

A finished lower level is planned around ceiling height, moisture and the location of the equipment and the main drain.

Arlington County issues building, plumbing and gas permits for the whole county.

Arlington County. There are no separate town permit offices in Arlington.
