Howard County permits and Columbia covenants

Two different reviews can apply. An interior-only project may need only the County permit.

  • Permits. Howard County’s Department of Inspections, Licenses and Permits issues building, plumbing, electrical and mechanical permits.
  • Village covenants. If the property is subject to a Columbia village’s architectural covenants, exterior alterations go to that village for approval first. The Columbia Association village list links each village.
  • What we handle. We include the permit steps in the written scope and flag any exterior item that needs village approval.

Who issues remodeling permits in Columbia?

Howard County’s Department of Inspections, Licenses and Permits. Columbia is not an incorporated city.

Do Columbia covenants apply to an interior remodel?

The village covenants deal with exterior alterations. If your property is subject to them and the project adds a window or changes a door, that item goes to your village first; see the village list.

Can we open the kitchen to the family room?

It depends on the wall. A site visit confirms whether the wall carries load; if it does, a beam is designed and included in the permit drawings.
