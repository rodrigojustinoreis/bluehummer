Which office covers a Laurel address

A Laurel mailing address can fall in the City or in one of several counties.

  • City of Laurel. The City issues permits for work inside city limits; see its interior renovations page. Exterior changes in its historic district go to the Historic District Commission first.
  • Prince George’s County. Outside the City, DPIE issues alteration permits, and plumbing and gas go through WSSC Water.
  • Howard or Anne Arundel County. Parts of the Laurel area are in these counties, which have their own permit offices; in Howard County it is DILP. We confirm by parcel.

Who issues the permit for a Laurel address?

It depends on the parcel: the City of Laurel inside city limits, otherwise Prince George’s, Howard or Anne Arundel County. We check it first.

Does WSSC handle plumbing in Laurel?

In the City and in Prince George’s County, plumbing and gas go through WSSC Water. The Howard and Anne Arundel parts are not WSSC areas.
