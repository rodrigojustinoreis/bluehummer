Ellicott City ranges from the historic district along Main Street to newer neighborhoods west and north of it. From our base in Olney we remodel kitchens, bathrooms and lower levels here, and we check early whether a property is historic.

Permits and the historic district

Howard County reviews the work; historic properties add one more step for exterior changes.

  • Permits. Howard County’s Department of Inspections, Licenses and Permits issues building, plumbing, electrical and mechanical permits.
  • Historic district. Exterior changes to properties in Ellicott City’s historic district are reviewed by the County’s Historic Preservation Commission.
  • Interior work. Remodeling inside the house follows the normal County permit path.

Does the historic district affect an interior remodel?

The Historic Preservation Commission reviews exterior changes in the district. Interior work follows the County permit process.

Who issues remodeling permits in Ellicott City?

Howard County’s Department of Inspections, Licenses and Permits.

What if we find old pipes or wiring?

It can happen in older homes. The written scope says how found conditions are handled, so a surprise does not stop the project.
