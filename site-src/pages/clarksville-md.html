Home remodeling in Clarksville, MD.

Clarksville is in western Howard County, close to our base in Olney. We plan kitchens, primary bathrooms and lower levels here, and the first visit includes a look at how the house is served: public utilities, or a well and septic system.

A larger kitchen can hold an island, a pantry and a coffee or beverage area. Each one adds its own plumbing, power and lighting, so they are planned together.

Bathroom remodeling

In a primary bath, decide whether a larger shower, a double vanity or better light matters most. Floor heat is decided before the tile.

Howard County permits, wells and septic

Howard County is the permit office; septic adds a health review for some projects.

  • Permits. Howard County’s Department of Inspections, Licenses and Permits issues building, plumbing, electrical and mechanical permits.
  • Well or septic. For homes on a well or septic system, the County Health Department requires a floor plan with some permit applications. Its floor plan memo explains what it needs.
  • Tell us early. Say whether the house is on public utilities or a well and septic system.

Who issues permits in Clarksville?

Howard County’s Department of Inspections, Licenses and Permits.

Does septic matter for a lower-level bathroom?

It can. The County Health Department reviews floor plans for some projects on a well or septic system; see its memo.

Can you plan more than one room at once?

Yes. One consultation can set the order of the kitchen, the bath and the lower level.
