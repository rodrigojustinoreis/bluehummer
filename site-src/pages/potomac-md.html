Permits & local specifics

What is different about remodeling in Potomac.

  • Montgomery County DPS permits. Potomac is unincorporated, so the Department of Permitting Services reviews structural, plumbing and electrical work. Larger projects that add square footage also go through zoning review for lot coverage and setbacks.
  • Septic capacity. A large share of Potomac west of Falls Road is on septic. Adding bedrooms, and sometimes bathrooms, requires a capacity check through DPS’s Well and Septic Section; some older systems need upgrading before a permit issues.
  • HOA architectural review. Avenel, River Falls and several newer enclaves review exterior changes such as new windows, doors and vent terminations. We prepare the submission alongside the County permit.
  • Electrical service. Pro-style appliances, steam showers, heated floors and EV charging add up. We confirm the existing service size early and include an upgrade in the scope when it is needed.

Do you work with architects and interior designers?

Yes, gladly. Many Potomac projects arrive with an architect’s drawings or a designer’s palette. We price from those documents, coordinate details during construction and keep everyone on one decision log. If you do not have a design team, we provide the design work ourselves.

Can you source natural stone slabs and custom cabinetry?

Yes. We take you to slab yards and cabinet showrooms around Montgomery County to select the actual quartzite, marble or soapstone slabs and to review door styles, finishes and interior fittings before anything is ordered.

Our house is on septic. Does that limit a basement guest suite?

It can. The bedroom count on your septic permit sets the limit. We review the record with you first; sometimes a den without a closet, or a system upgrade, is the right answer.

How far is Potomac from your base?

About 25 minutes from Olney via the ICC and Falls Road. Our crew is on site daily for the length of the project.
