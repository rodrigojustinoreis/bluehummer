The County and the Town of Vienna

Vienna is the reverse of some towns: the County issues the building permit.

  • Building permit. Fairfax County is the building official for the Town of Vienna and issues building permits through Land Development Services.
  • Town review. The Town’s Planning and Zoning department reviews the County application for compliance with the Town Zoning Code. Its residential projects page explains the order.
  • Before any agreement. We confirm that we can take a project at your address, including what that jurisdiction requires of contractors.

Who issues building permits in Vienna, VA?

Fairfax County. The Town of Vienna then reviews the application for zoning; see the Town’s residential projects page.

Do I need a permit to finish the basement?

Yes. Fairfax County requires an interior alteration permit and an electrical permit; see its finished basement page.

Do you work in Vienna?

It is inside our service area. We confirm each address and what Virginia requires before any agreement.
