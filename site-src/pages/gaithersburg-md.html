City of Gaithersburg or the County?

A Gaithersburg mailing address does not always mean the City reviews the work, so the address is checked first.

  • Inside city limits. The City of Gaithersburg issues residential improvement permits for work such as interior alterations.
  • Outside city limits. Montgomery County’s Department of Permitting Services issues the building permit, and plumbing and gas go through WSSC Water.
  • What we handle. We tell you which office applies to your address and include the permit steps in the written scope.

Does the City of Gaithersburg issue my permit?

Only if the house is inside the city limits. A Gaithersburg mailing address can belong to a house in unincorporated Montgomery County, where the County Department of Permitting Services is the office.

Can a kitchen wall come down?

It depends on the wall. Whether it carries load is checked on site before anything is drawn. If it does, the opening is designed with a beam and becomes part of the permit drawings.

Can you add a bathroom in the basement?

It depends on where the main drain sits and how deep it is. If the drain is too high for gravity, a pump is planned. We look at it on the first visit.

Do you take smaller jobs first?

Yes. A small repair can be a first project, and a larger remodel can follow when you are ready.
