Older Bethesda houses, county permits.

Much of Bethesda is colonials and Cape Cods built between the 1930s and the 1960s, with mid-century modern houses in Carderock Springs.

  • Montgomery County DPS issues the building permits. Bethesda is unincorporated, so structural and electrical work on a kitchen, bath or basement is permitted and inspected by the Department of Permitting Services. Plumbing and gas permits come from WSSC Water.
  • Some addresses add a second layer. Several municipalities and historic districts in the Bethesda and Chevy Chase corridor add their own review on top of the County’s, mostly for exterior changes. We confirm what applies to your address during planning.
  • Mid-century details worth keeping. In Carderock Springs, original features such as a beam ceiling are planned around, not torn out.
