Services by room

Kitchen remodeling

Where the sink, the range and the refrigerator sit decides how the room works. A kitchen can keep its layout and get new cabinets and counters, or the layout itself can change.

Bathroom remodeling

Hall baths, primary suites and powder rooms. The waterproofing behind the tile and the position of each fixture are decided before demolition.

Basements and interiors

Finishing a lower level as a family room, guest suite, office or media room, and remodeling interior rooms upstairs. Moisture, ceiling height and daylight are checked before a layout is drawn.

We also take on small repairs around the house. A repair can be a first project with us, planned and written up the same way. Ask about a small repair

How a project is planned

Design first, then a written scope, then construction. See our remodeling process

  1. Conversation and walkthrough

    Your goals, your home’s conditions and a realistic budget range.

  2. Design and selections

    Layouts and drawings first, then cabinetry and finishes chosen together.

  3. Scope, price and permits

    A written proposal, with the permits your address requires confirmed before work is accepted.

  4. Build and walk-through

    The work follows the agreed scope and ends with a walk-through of the finished room.
