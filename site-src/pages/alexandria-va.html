City permits and the historic district

For qualifying exterior changes, historic review comes before the permit.

  • City of Alexandria. The City’s Permit Center handles building permits. When exterior work on a property in a historic district requires Board of Architectural Review approval, that approval is obtained before the related permit application; see the City’s submittal requirements.
  • Interior work. In the Old and Historic Alexandria District, interior work does not need Board approval; exterior changes visible from the public right-of-way do. See historic preservation.
  • Outside the City. Alexandria mailing addresses outside the city limits are in Fairfax County, where Land Development Services issues permits. We confirm by parcel.
  • Before any agreement. We confirm that we can take a project at your address, including what that jurisdiction requires of contractors.

Does the historic district affect an interior remodel?

Not for interior work. The City’s Board of Architectural Review covers exterior changes visible from the public right-of-way; see historic preservation.

Who issues the permit for an Alexandria address?

Inside the city limits, the City of Alexandria. Outside them, Fairfax County. The parcel decides it, not the mailing address.

Do you work in Alexandria?

It is inside our service area. We confirm each address and what Virginia requires before any agreement.
