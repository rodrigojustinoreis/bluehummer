The address decides the permit office

A mailing address can say one city while the house sits in another jurisdiction. Each city page names the office for that area; we confirm it for your parcel.

  • Montgomery County. The County Department of Permitting Services in most places; the cities of Rockville and Gaithersburg issue their own, and some towns add a step.
  • Howard County. The Department of Inspections, Licenses and Permits covers the whole county.
  • Nearby Maryland. The City of Frederick, the City of Laurel and Baltimore County each have their own office; see Frederick, Laurel and Owings Mills.
  • Washington, DC and Northern Virginia. The District, each county and the independent cities have their own offices, and some towns add a zoning step. Whether we can take a project there is confirmed for that jurisdiction before any agreement.

What to send with your address

Four details are enough to tell whether the project is one we can take.

  • The street address. It shows which county, city or town reviews the work.
  • The room and the change you want. A kitchen, a bathroom, a basement or another interior room, or a small repair.
  • Water and sewer. Say whether the house is on public service or has a well or a septic system.
  • Budget range and timing. Both shape the scope from the first conversation.
