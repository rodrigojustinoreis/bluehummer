Who issues the permit depends on where you live.

Most kitchen, bath and basement projects that move walls, plumbing, electrical or structure need a residential building permit. We handle the paperwork, but the office changes by address.

  • Montgomery County DPS. Olney, Potomac, Bethesda, Chevy Chase, Kensington and all unincorporated areas. Plumbing and gas permits come from WSSC Water.
  • City of Rockville. Homes inside Rockville city limits use the City’s Inspection Services Division, not the County.
  • Howard County DILP. Clarksville, Highland, Dayton, Glenelg, Glenwood, Fulton, West Friendship and Ellicott City.
  • Municipal and historic layers. The Town of Chevy Chase, Chevy Chase Village, Kensington, Garrett Park and Brookeville add their own permits or historic review for many exterior changes. We confirm this during planning so nothing surprises you later.
