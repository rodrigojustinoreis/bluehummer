Who reviews a remodel in North Potomac

North Potomac is unincorporated Montgomery County.

  • Building permit. The County Department of Permitting Services reviews interior alterations. Plumbing and gas go through WSSC Water where the home is on public water and sewer.
  • Septic systems. If the house has a septic system, the County checks it before additions or improvements that add bedrooms or plumbing. Its well and septic guide explains when.
  • Tell us early. Say whether the house is on public sewer or septic. It can change what a basement bath involves.

Does a septic system affect a basement bathroom?

It can. The County reviews septic capacity for some improvements that add plumbing or bedrooms. Its well and septic guide lists the cases.

Keep the tub or add a bigger shower?

It depends on who uses the room. If another bathroom already has a tub, you may choose to use this room for a larger shower, subject to the layout.

Who issues the permit in North Potomac?

Montgomery County’s Department of Permitting Services. North Potomac has no city or town permit office.
