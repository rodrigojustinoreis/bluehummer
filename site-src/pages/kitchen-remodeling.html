Can you remodel a kitchen without changing the entire floor plan?

Possibly. A focused refresh or partial redesign can sometimes achieve meaningful improvements; we assess the scope before recommending a path.

Can you help choose cabinetry and finishes?

Yes, if it is part of the agreed scope. Cabinet styles come first, then the surfaces and fixtures that go with them.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

It depends on the scope, permits, product availability, and existing conditions. A timeline is discussed after the project is defined.
