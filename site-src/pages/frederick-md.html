City of Frederick or Frederick County?

The City issues its own permits; the County covers the rest.

  • Inside the City. The City of Frederick Building Department issues building permits inside city limits.
  • Historic district. In the City’s historic district, exterior changes visible from a public way need approval from the Historic Preservation Commission before permits.
  • Outside the City. Frederick County Planning and Permitting issues permits for most of the rest of the county.
  • Address first. Frederick is toward the edge of our area, so each address is confirmed before planning.

Who issues the permit for a Frederick address?

Inside city limits, the City of Frederick. Elsewhere, Frederick County Planning and Permitting.

Does the historic district affect an interior remodel?

The Historic Preservation Commission reviews exterior changes visible from a public way. Interior work follows the City permit process.
