Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Blue Hammer Remodeling respects the information you share when asking about a residential remodeling project.

Information we collect

Our consultation forms may collect your name, email address, telephone number, project ZIP code, type of remodeling project, preferred timeline, and any additional details you submit. We also collect basic technical information needed to operate and protect the website.

How we use your information

We use the information to review inquiries, communicate about potential projects, assess our service area, and maintain records of requests. We do not claim to sell your personal information.

Where requests are stored

In the included server implementation, form submissions are recorded on the private server in a restricted-access local data file. If email notifications are configured, the lead details are also sent to a business inbox using the selected email provider.

Analytics and third-party content

The starter website does not include third-party ad trackers. Illustrative stock photos may be requested from Unsplash when a visitor's browser can access that service. If additional tracking or marketing tools are installed, this policy should be updated before use.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect inquiries, but no system can guarantee absolute security. The business should establish a retention and deletion practice appropriate to its operations and applicable law.

Contact and corrections

If you would like to ask about your information, use our contact page. Actual business contact details should be added to the website before public launch.
