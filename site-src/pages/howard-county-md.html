The homes we see here

Four kinds of Howard County houses, four kinds of projects.

  • River Hill and Clarksville (21029). Columbia’s westernmost village, built mostly in the 1990s: large colonials with two-story family rooms, first-floor offices and big unfinished basements. Kitchens are due for an update from oak and laminate to painted cabinetry and stone; walkout basements are the second most common project.
  • Highland, Dayton, Glenelg, Glenwood and West Friendship. Three-acre-plus lots along Route 108, Route 97 and Triadelphia Road: 1980s–2000s customs and farmhouses on well and septic, with room for a scullery, a mudroom suite or a full in-law apartment on the lower level.
  • Fulton and Maple Lawn (20759). A 2000s–2010s traditional neighborhood with HOA design guidelines. Interiors are newer but builder-grade; owners want custom millwork, a better kitchen island and a finished basement that matches the main floor.
  • Ellicott City (21042). Turf Valley, Centennial, Dunloggin, Font Hill and Chatham: 1960s–90s colonials and split-levels, plus newer homes around Turf Valley, with the historic district downtown under separate review.

Permits & local specifics

Howard County is a different jurisdiction, and we plan for it.

  • Howard County DILP. Residential building, plumbing and electrical permits in Clarksville, Highland, Fulton, Glenelg and Ellicott City go through the Department of Inspections, Licenses and Permits, with its own forms, review times and inspection scheduling. We file and manage them.
  • Well and septic through the Health Department. Much of western Howard County is on private septic. Adding bedrooms or bathrooms may require a review by the Howard County Health Department before DILP issues the building permit.
  • Columbia Association covenants. In River Hill and other Columbia villages, exterior changes such as new windows, doors or vents need Architectural Committee approval through the village. Interior remodels generally do not, but we check.
  • Maple Lawn and other HOAs. Exterior work is reviewed against design guidelines. We prepare the application as part of the project.
  • Ellicott City historic district. Exterior changes to homes inside the historic district go before the Historic Preservation Commission in addition to DILP.

Do you really cover Howard County from Olney?

Yes. Clarksville is about 20 minutes from Olney via Route 108 and Fulton about 25 via Route 216. Highland, Dayton, Glenelg, Glenwood, West Friendship and Ellicott City are all inside our daily range, so the same crew is on site every day.

Who pulls the permit in Howard County?

We do, through Howard County DILP, and we coordinate any Health Department septic review, Columbia Association or HOA submission the project needs.

Can you finish a basement in a River Hill home with a bedroom?

Yes, provided there is a code-compliant egress window or walkout door for the bedroom and the septic record, if applicable, supports the added bedroom. Most River Hill homes are on public sewer, which simplifies it.

Can we keep our existing kitchen layout and just upgrade finishes?

Of course. A same-layout refresh with new cabinetry, counters, backsplash, lighting and appliances is a well-defined project and typically plans between $55,000 and $95,000 in this area.
