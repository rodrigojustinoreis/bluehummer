Town of Herndon or Fairfax County?

Unlike Vienna, the Town of Herndon issues its own building permits.

  • Inside the Town. The Town of Herndon’s Building Inspections division issues permits for alterations; see how to apply for a permit.
  • Outside the Town. Herndon mailing addresses outside town limits go to Fairfax County Land Development Services. We confirm by parcel.
