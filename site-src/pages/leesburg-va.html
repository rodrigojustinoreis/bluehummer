Town zoning, County permit, historic district

Inside the Town, there are two steps, and the historic district adds a third for exterior work.

  • Town zoning first. A Town of Leesburg zoning permit is needed before the County building permit is issued; see the Town’s community development FAQ.
  • County permit. Loudoun County Building and Development issues the building permit.
  • Historic district. In the Old and Historic District, exterior alterations need a Certificate of Appropriateness, even for in-kind replacement.

Why does Leesburg need a Town permit and a County permit?

Loudoun County issues the building permit, and the Town of Leesburg issues a zoning permit that must come first. See the Town’s FAQ.

Does the historic district affect an interior remodel?

The Old and Historic District rules cover exterior alterations; interior work follows the Town and County permit steps.
