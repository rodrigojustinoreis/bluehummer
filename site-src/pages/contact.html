What happens after I send the form?

We review your project and location, then reply to schedule a conversation. If the project and the address are a fit, the next step is a walkthrough of your home to go over your goals, the existing conditions and the factors that will shape the budget.

Is there any obligation?

None. Sending the form is a request, not a contract. Scope, price and schedule are agreed in writing before any work is accepted.

Do you work outside Montgomery and Howard County, MD?

The service area reaches about 40 miles from Olney, which takes in Washington, DC and part of Northern Virginia. Send your ZIP code with the request. Whether we can take a project there is confirmed for that jurisdiction before any agreement.
