City of Fairfax or Fairfax County?

The City of Fairfax is an independent city with its own permit office.

  • Inside the City. The City of Fairfax issues residential building permits; a zoning approval comes first. See the City’s residential building permit page.
  • Elsewhere in the County. Fairfax County Land Development Services issues permits. The County’s address tools help confirm where a property sits.
  • Before any agreement. We confirm that we can take a project at your address, including what that jurisdiction requires of contractors.
