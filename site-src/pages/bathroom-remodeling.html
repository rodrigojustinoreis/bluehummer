More Frequently Asked Questions {{ARROW}}
Can you update my bathroom without moving plumbing?

Depending on the existing layout, keeping fixtures in place may be a practical option. We would review your goals first.

Can you help with a small bathroom?

Yes, small bathrooms often benefit from recessed niches, wall-hung vanities and pocket doors, subject to scope and availability.

Do bathrooms require special materials?

Bathrooms need appropriately selected, installed and maintained water-resistant systems. Specific selections depend on the project.
