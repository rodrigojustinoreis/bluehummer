City of Falls Church or Fairfax County?

A Falls Church mailing address does not always mean the City.

  • Inside the City. The City of Falls Church Building Safety Division issues permits. It lists when one is needed, such as changing a wall or moving plumbing or electrical fixtures: when a permit is required.
  • Outside the City. Falls Church addresses outside the city limits are in Fairfax County, where Land Development Services issues permits. We confirm by parcel.
  • Before any agreement. We confirm that we can take a project at your address, including what that jurisdiction requires of contractors.

Who issues the permit for a Falls Church address?

Inside the city limits, the City of Falls Church. Outside them, Fairfax County Land Development Services.

Do new cabinets need a permit?

The City says painting, flooring and cabinet replacement generally do not; changing walls or moving fixtures does. See its permit page.

Do you work in Falls Church?

It is inside our service area. We confirm each address and what Virginia requires before any agreement.
