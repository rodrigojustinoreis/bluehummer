Who reviews a remodel in Clarksburg

Clarksburg is unincorporated Montgomery County.

  • Building permit. The County Department of Permitting Services reviews interior alterations, and plumbing and gas go through WSSC Water.
  • Historic district. Clarksburg has a designated historic district. Exterior changes to properties in it are reviewed through a Historic Area Work Permit; the Planning map shows which properties are included.
  • HOA rules. If your community has an association, check whether it reviews exterior items. Its rules are separate from the permit.
