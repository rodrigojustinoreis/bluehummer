Who issues remodeling permits in McLean?

Fairfax County Land Development Services. McLean is not an incorporated town.

Does replacing cabinets need a permit?

Fairfax County says replacing cabinets or countertops does not, but moving plumbing, electrical or gas does. Its permit page lists the cases.
