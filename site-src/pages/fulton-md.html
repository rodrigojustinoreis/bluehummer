Home remodeling in Fulton, MD.

Fulton is in southern Howard County, within our service area from Olney. We remodel kitchens, bathrooms and lower levels, from updating finishes in the same layout to reworking a room around a new one.

Same layout or a new one

The biggest difference in cost comes from whether walls and pipes move.

Kitchen remodeling

Same layout: new cabinets, counters, lighting and appliances. New layout: the sink, range or island moves, which adds plumbing, gas and electrical work.

Bathroom remodeling

Fixtures in place: new tile, vanity and shower. Fixtures moved: new drain and supply lines, a rebuilt shower and sometimes a reframed wall.

Basements and interiors

One open room is the simpler finish. Separate rooms with a bath add plumbing, doors, wiring and heating and cooling for each room.

Who reviews a remodel in Fulton

Fulton is unincorporated Howard County.

  • Permits. Howard County’s Department of Inspections, Licenses and Permits issues building, plumbing, electrical and mechanical permits.
  • HOA rules. If your community has an association, check whether it reviews exterior items. Its rules are separate from the permit.
  • Address first. We confirm the address is inside our service area before planning.
