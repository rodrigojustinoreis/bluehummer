Who reviews a remodel in Germantown

Germantown is not an incorporated city, so there is no separate town office to add.

  • Building permit. Montgomery County’s Department of Permitting Services reviews interior alterations such as moving walls, plumbing or electrical work.
  • Plumbing and gas. These permits go through WSSC Water.
  • Historic district. Germantown has a County historic district. Exterior changes to a property in it need a Historic Area Work Permit; the Planning map shows which properties are designated.

Who issues the permit for a Germantown remodel?

Montgomery County’s Department of Permitting Services, with plumbing and gas through WSSC Water. Germantown has no city permit office.

Can we remodel in phases?

Yes. A phased plan decides the order up front, for example the kitchen first and the basement later, so the first phase does not have to be undone.

Is a walk-in shower a big change?

If it replaces a tub in the same spot, the drain moves only slightly. The bigger items are the waterproofing, the glass and the tile.

What should I send before a consultation?

The address, the room, a few photos and a line on your timing. A budget range helps us suggest the right scope.
