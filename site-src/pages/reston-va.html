Does Reston Association review an interior remodel?

Its Design Review Board reviews exterior alterations. Interior work goes through the County permit process.

Who issues remodeling permits in Reston?

Fairfax County Land Development Services. Reston is not an incorporated town.

Do you work in Reston?

It is inside our service area. We confirm each address and what Virginia requires before any agreement.
