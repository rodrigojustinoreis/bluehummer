Frequently asked questions

The details homeowners want to know.

What types of remodeling projects do you handle?

Our core focus is kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, and basements and interiors. Share your project details so we can confirm whether the scope is a good fit.

How much does a remodel cost?

Pricing depends on size, materials, existing conditions, and the work required. We start with a project conversation and determine what information is needed for a realistic estimate.

How soon can my project start?

Timing depends on your project, material lead times, and our availability. Tell us your preferred timeframe and we will discuss the next steps.

Do I need to have a finished design already?

No. You can reach out with a clear plan, early ideas, or a list of problems you want to solve.

What areas do you serve?

We focus on homeowners in Montgomery County, Maryland, including Rockville, Bethesda, Silver Spring, Potomac, Gaithersburg, Olney, and nearby communities. Service availability is confirmed by address.

Will permits be needed for my project?

Permit requirements vary by scope and jurisdiction. We review this during project planning and confirm the appropriate requirements before work is accepted.
