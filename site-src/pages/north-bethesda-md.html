A Rockville mailing address is not always Rockville

North Bethesda is unincorporated, but a home here can carry a Rockville mailing address.

  • Unincorporated North Bethesda. Montgomery County’s Department of Permitting Services issues the building permit, and plumbing and gas go through WSSC Water.
  • Inside the City of Rockville. If the house is within city limits, the City issues the permits. The City’s address finder confirms which side of the line you are on.
  • Before the plan. We confirm the office for your address so the drawings follow the right review.

My address says Rockville. Who issues the permit?

It depends on whether the house is inside the City of Rockville. North Bethesda itself is unincorporated, where the County Department of Permitting Services issues the permit.

Island or peninsula?

An island needs a clear walkway on every side, so it suits a wider room. A peninsula gives similar counter space where the room is narrower.

Can a small bathroom feel bigger without moving walls?

Yes. A wall-hung vanity, a glass panel instead of a curtain, larger tile and better lighting all help.
