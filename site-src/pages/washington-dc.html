DC permits and historic districts

The District has its own permit office, separate from Maryland and Virginia.

  • Permits. The DC Department of Buildings issues alteration and repair permits for work on existing homes, including reconfiguring space. Its Homeowner’s Center helps owners of one- and two-family homes.
  • Historic districts. When permitted work changes the exterior of a historic property, the Historic Preservation Office reviews it as part of the permit. Interior-only work does not trigger that review on its own.

Who issues remodeling permits in Washington, DC?

The DC Department of Buildings. Its alteration and repair permit covers renovation of an existing structure.

Does a historic district affect an interior remodel?

Not on its own. The Historic Preservation Office reviews permitted work that affects the exterior of a historic property.

Do you take every DC address?
